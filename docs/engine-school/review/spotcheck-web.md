# Web spot-check: 20 skills

Checked 2026-11-13. Five skills per file; assessment covers both principle and rule against the cited locator. **Supported** means the substantive rule follows the source; **partly** means the source supports the direction but not all mandatory details or scope; **not supported** means it contradicts or fails to substantiate the central claim; **could not open** means the cited content could not be inspected. Practical acceptance checks can be defensible house rules without being source requirements. Search-indexed text is identified where the actual page blocked access. The sources below are the URLs cited by the skills unless explicitly identified as supplemental.

## Lov

1. **lov-no-dokumentasjon-for-publisering — partly.** [Mfl. § 3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-01-09-2), indexed text: factual claims must be documented and the advertiser must hold documentation when marketing occurs; a separate per-claim reference and dated control record are additional checks.
2. **lov-no-synlig-reklame — partly.** [Mfl. § 8 and § 28](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-01-09-2), indexed text: commercial intent must not be misleadingly omitted and § 28 requires marketing to be clearly recognizable as marketing; the universal before-the-sales-message test is a stricter operationalization. [Forbrukertilsynet](https://www.forbrukertilsynet.no/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/orienteringsbrev-til-annonsorer.pdf) cites the former § 3 for recognizable advertising; the current locator is § 28, so the older guidance must be read with the renumbering in mind.
3. **lov-eu-ekte-tidsfrist — partly.** [UCPD Annex I(7)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02005L0029-20260927) prohibits falsely claiming a very limited period to force an immediate decision; a campaign-plan match and per-recipient reset test are additional controls and do not alone establish a true deadline.
4. **lov-mat-godkjent-helsepastand — partly.** [Regulation 1924/2006 art. 10(1)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02006R1924-20141213) sets the authorization and conditions baseline; the categorical failure for all unapproved claims omits potentially applicable art. 28 transitional measures, confirmed in [CJEU C-386/23, para. 66](https://infocuria.curia.europa.eu/tabs/redirect/juris/documents.jsf?critereEcli=ECLI%3AEU%3AC%3A2025%3A304).
5. **lov-mat-generell-helsefordel — partly.** [Art. 10(3)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02006R1924-20141213) requires a specific listed claim alongside general health benefits; the rigid listed-claim requirement needs the same narrowly applicable art. 28 transitional qualification ([CJEU C-386/23](https://infocuria.curia.europa.eu/tabs/redirect/juris/documents.jsf?critereEcli=ECLI%3AEU%3AC%3A2025%3A304)).

## Strategi

11. **strategi-lokal-vekstevidens — partly.** [Ehrenberg-Bass, Laws of Growth Analysis](https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/laws-of-growth-analysis), opened with curl: one purpose is checking whether growth laws apply to the category and country; named planned studies and pass/fail documentation are additional requirements.
12. **strategi-lette-kjopere — partly.** [Ehrenberg-Bass, Identify how your brand competes](https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/laws-of-growth-analysis), opened with curl, explicitly lists prevalence of light buyers; it does not prescribe the lowest positive frequency group or measurement period as a gate.
13. **strategi-kortsiktig-og-langsiktig-maling — partly.** [IPA / Binet & Field, principle 10](https://ipa.co.uk/knowledge/documents/the-long-and-the-short-of-it-presentation) says to measure short- and long-term effects; baseline, source, deadline and exact relative duration are extra operational choices.
14. **strategi-pris-og-volum — partly.** [IPA / Binet & Field, principle 1](https://ipa.co.uk/knowledge/documents/the-long-and-the-short-of-it-presentation) says to support volume and price, but the cited summary does not require decomposition and separate measures for every chosen revenue target.
15. **strategi-samspill-merke-og-salg — partly.** [IPA / Binet & Field, principle 8](https://ipa.co.uk/knowledge/documents/the-long-and-the-short-of-it-presentation) calls for integrating brand and activation; the required shared element and offer in both drafts are an additional implementation choice.

## Leseren

16. **leseren-begrunn-kunnskapsnivaa — partly.** [Copp's explanation of Schwartz, five levels](https://www.destinicopp.com/blog/eugene-schwartz-five-levels-of-awareness) describes the five levels, including awareness and desire for the product; a documented observation or labeled assumption is an added procedure.
17. **leseren-ukjent-problem — partly.** [Copp, five levels](https://www.destinicopp.com/blog/eugene-schwartz-five-levels-of-awareness) defines unaware readers as not seeing a problem; it does not establish a mandatory everyday situation or symptom before the first CTA.
18. **leseren-kjent-problem — partly.** [Copp, five levels](https://www.destinicopp.com/blog/eugene-schwartz-five-levels-of-awareness) says problem-aware readers know something is wrong but not that a fix exists; naming the problem and solution type before the first CTA is an added rule.
19. **leseren-klar-for-kjoep — partly.** [Copp, five levels](https://www.destinicopp.com/blog/eugene-schwartz-five-levels-of-awareness) says the most aware know and want the product and need price and link; requiring product name, price access and purchase link before every background story is a stricter gate.
20. **leseren-tre-sider-av-jobben — partly.** [Christensen Institute, infographic](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/) identifies functional, social and emotional forces; separate reader-card fields and evidence/unknown labels are additional safeguards.
