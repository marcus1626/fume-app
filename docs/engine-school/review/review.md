# Fume School — independent review

13 November 2026. All recommendations are **proposed**. Scope: `BRIEF.md`, all 77 entries in `sources/pensum.json`, all 220 skills in six subject files, and all 13 flow files plus `flows/_index.json`. Python parsed every JSON file, enumerated IDs, sources, quotes, conflict declarations, step validators and handoffs, and compared rule text for possible overlap. Similarity results were manually interpreted, not treated as proof of duplication. Source checks below actually read the cited local Hopkins text; this is a focused spot check, not verification of every URL, law or current platform specification. No test of a running Engine was possible from these declarative files.

## 1. Duplicates and near-duplicates between skills

- `skills/Plattform.json` **plattform-ingen-ki-ugc-eller-falske-kunder** and `skills/Lov.json` **lov-fume-ingen-ai-ugc** prohibit the same two practices and fail on unknown provenance. The former is pre-publication and explicitly checks all depictions and UGC; the latter refers to published assets. Keep one canonical rule with a pre-publication scope and reference it from both contexts, rather than risk different outcomes.
- `skills/Copy.json` **copy-mellomtittel-navngir-innhold** and `skills/Leseren.json` **leseren-overskrifter-for-skumming** require the same informative subheading. The reader skill is scoped to information pages; consolidate or explicitly make that scope an application of the general rule.
- `skills/Copy.json` **copy-les-hoeyt-og-rett** and **copy-menneskelig-korrektur** overlap on logged human correction. Reading aloud is an additional method, so either combine with a conditional read-aloud check or state why both are independently required.
- `skills/Idé.json` **ide-bildets-bidrag**, **ide-metafor-med-forbindelse**, and **ide-symbol-med-betydning** overlap in asking for an explained visual meaning. They are not identical: metaphor and repeated symbol are narrower cases. Make the general-to-specific relationship explicit to avoid multiple identical failures.
- `skills/Strategi.json` **strategi-respons-er-ikke-salg** and **strategi-kundeverdi-fremfor-svar** both distinguish response from actual commercial outcomes; the latter also asks to follow responses to customer value. Keep the distinct measurement requirement, but cross-reference and avoid counting the same failure twice.

## 2. Uncheckable or inadequately operationalised rules

These have apparent pass/fail language, but the specified evidence or decision procedure can still allow arbitrary results.

- `skills/Copy.json` **copy-svar-paa-kjoepsspoersmaal**: the producer chooses which questions “must” be answered; an empty or selectively incomplete list can pass. Require a nonempty, reader-grounded list and independent completeness review.
- `skills/Copy.json` **copy-uavhengig-av-forrige-annonse**: “necessary context” has no defined minimum. Specify offer, audience situation, material qualification and next action as applicable, and assign a critic or human to judge sufficiency.
- `skills/Idé.json` **ide-bildets-bidrag**: naming a supposed additional interpretation passes even if the image does not communicate it. Require an independent critic to identify the visual evidence and a negative finding when the claimed meaning is absent; audience testing is useful where feasible.
- `skills/Strategi.json` **strategi-prospektdekning**: every omitted group only needs a “justification”; any assertion could pass. Require evidence or a marked assumption and a decision on whether the commercial or legal constraint supports exclusion.
- `skills/Plattform.json` **plattform-tiktok-fungerende-lyd**: “a listener” can repeat every spoken claim, but the listener, test conditions and treatment of inaudible or absent speech are not defined. Record an independent listener, device/volume, transcript and pass criteria, with a no-speech branch.
- `skills/Copy.json` **copy-temaord-tidlig-i-titler** is mechanically countable only if a human or critic decides which word expresses the topic. Its unconditional two-word position also blocks useful headings without demonstrating better comprehension. Treat as an optional heuristic, not a universal pass/fail gate.

## 4. Unrecorded conflicts and tensions

All 220 `conflicts_with` arrays are empty. The following are context-dependent tensions, not proof that both rules can never be applied together; record each pair and specify priority and exceptions.

- `skills/Strategi.json` **strategi-prospektdekning** / **strategi-fremtidige-kjopere** versus `skills/Copy.json` **copy-overskrift-viser-relevans**: broad category reach and future buyers can be narrowed by making every individual headline speak to the specific reader-card situation. Preserve reach in the media plan and vary creative across situations; do not infer that narrow targeting is mandatory. Record reciprocal links.
- `skills/Strategi.json` **strategi-fremtidige-kjopere** / **strategi-kortsiktig-og-langsiktig-maling** versus **strategi-salgsutlosende-informasjon**: an always-on demand-capture interpretation favours current intent and immediate response. Scope the latter to explicitly selected activation, preserve separately budgeted long-term reach and measurement, and cross-link.
- `skills/Strategi.json` **strategi-ett-budskap-per-annonse** versus `skills/Copy.json` **copy-svar-paa-kjoepsspoersmaal** and **copy-uavhengig-av-forrige-annonse**: a one-situation main message and a complete stand-alone offer can compete for scarce ad space. Distinguish main creative message from necessary supporting information and allow an accessible landing page where appropriate.
- `skills/Plattform.json` **plattform-shorts-ekte-produktbruk** / **plattform-tiktok-staaende-video** versus **plattform-ingen-ki-ugc-eller-falske-kunder** and `skills/Lov.json` **lov-fume-ingen-ai-ugc**: no direct contradiction in the present text; authentic creator-style production may be misread as permission for synthetic UGC. Record the potential platform-advice/ethics tension and make Fume's ban explicit at the relevant use point. Do not claim the platform rules themselves currently require prohibited content.

## 5. Missing principles or flows

- `sources/pensum.json` **Ehrenberg-Bass / Byron Sharp — Laws of Growth** and `skills/Strategi.json` **strategi-prospektdekning**: the school asks for category coverage, but does not require a quantified reach/frequency or overlap plan against that audience, nor a way to compare actual delivery with it. Add a conditional reach/measurement skill and a media-planning flow with assumptions, constraints and data sources.
- `sources/pensum.json` **Behavioural Insights Team — EAST framework** and `skills/Leseren.json` **leseren-kartlegg-friksjon**: friction is inventoried, but no flow systematically maps observed barriers to easy, attractive, social and timely interventions and then tests them. Add an optional intervention flow tied to evidence, consent and measurement; do not present all four as mandatory in every ad.
- `flows/testplan-ekte-annonser.json` **testplan-ekte-annonser** ends with a plan, not actual execution, result ingestion, uncertainty-aware analysis or a decision log. Add a post-launch measurement/learning flow with a human-controlled launch and privacy-safe data handling. This is an additional opportunity, not an unmet requirement that the current flow itself launch ads.
- `flows/merkevare-og-salgsaktivering.json` **merkevare-og-salgsaktivering** is optional although brand versus activation is a central curriculum tension. Require an explicit applicability decision in `flows/_index.json`, especially when both horizons exist; do not impose a universal budget ratio.

## 6. Flow validation, critic independence, chains and skill fit

- `flows/klarsprak-revisjon.json` **klarsprak-revisjon**, step 2: `validation.who` is `kode`, yet the checks include whether each buying question is answered, whether meaning is unchanged and whether test tasks reveal the answer. Mere field presence cannot establish these semantic facts. Step 3 provides a critic later, but step 2 must pass before that critic can read it. Split structural checks from semantic checks and move semantic decisions to the independent critic or human.
- `flows/logikkjede.json` **logikkjede**, step 3, and `flows/merkevare-og-salgsaktivering.json` **merkevare-og-salgsaktivering**, step 4: the critic writes its own judgment and code is named as validator of substantive claims such as support for the brief or a locally justified budget. Code can check the judgment and evidence fields, not the truth of the judgment. Specify what is independently reviewed and route unresolved or unsupported conclusions to a human.
- `flows/kanalformat-tilpasning.json` **kanalformat-tilpasning**, step 4: code must verify every placement has allowed dimensions, duration and file format, but the source and version of the applicable placement specifications are not prescribed. An unversioned or stale lookup can certify an invalid asset. Require a dated, placement-specific specification and a human fallback for unavailable/ambiguous rules.
- `flows/_index.json` **model_rule**, and all critic steps: every flow explicitly requires actual provider/model IDs to differ from every contributing writer model and routes to a human if unavailable. I found no declared critic step permitting the same model. However, these are instructions, not a demonstrated runtime enforcement mechanism: require trusted execution metadata and fail closed when model identity cannot be attested. Different models also do not ensure independent evidence or correct criticism.
- `flows/_index.json` **flows** and **optional_routes**: the standard nine-flow input/output paths form a produced chain, with the externally supplied `oppdrag.json` explicitly documented. The four optional routes have explicit rebinding, including the combined plain-language/channel route. No definitely orphaned input artifact found in the declared routes. This is a static path check, not a runtime integration test; the artifact contract still lacks machine-readable schemas and proof that all carried fields exist.
- `flows/merkevare-og-salgsaktivering.json` **merkevare-og-salgsaktivering** lists `skills/Plattform.json` **plattform-ingen-ki-ugc-eller-falske-kunder**, and `flows/brief-en-setning.json` **brief-en-setning** lists `skills/Lov.json` **lov-fume-ingen-ai-ugc**, even for text-only planning. A universal ethics gate is appropriate, but calling it a flow-specific skill without an applicability branch makes every text artifact look like an asset-provenance audit. Move it to the global gate with a documented “no relevant media” result, or justify its inclusion explicitly.
- `flows/klarsprak-revisjon.json` **klarsprak-revisjon**, step 4, says the comprehension test precedes final execution approval, while its declared input is already `09-annonseutforelse-godkjent.json`. Clarify that the earlier approval is a provisional creative approval and the revised version cannot receive final release approval until retested and legally checked.

## 7. Copyright and Fume-ethics risks

- `sources/pensum.json` **Claude Hopkins — Scientific Advertising** and **My Life in Advertising** / `sources/scientific-advertising.txt` and `sources/my-life-in-advertising.txt`: the brief treats these as public domain, but status can vary by jurisdiction, edition and translation. Record the exact edition, provenance and territory before redistribution; source availability alone is not a copyright clearance. No conclusion here that the present copies are infringing.
- `flows/_index.json` **handoff_rule** and each flow's `validation.artifact_contract`: carrying the entire approved basis and embedded media forward at each step can replicate third-party material or personal data throughout the chain. Store references and minimum necessary excerpts/media, record permissions and retention, and preserve auditability without copying full protected works.
- `skills/Plattform.json` **plattform-ingen-ki-ugc-eller-falske-kunder** and `skills/Lov.json` **lov-fume-ingen-ai-ugc**: self-reported provenance is not proof. Require source files, production records and accountable human review when provenance is uncertain; unknown must remain a failure. The Fume ban is broader than a disclosure requirement and cannot be waived by labelling.
- `flows/klarsprak-revisjon.json` **klarsprak-revisjon** step 4 and `flows/innsiktsjakt.json` **innsiktsjakt**: interviews and test responses can contain sensitive personal information. Define consent or other applicable basis, minimisation, access and retention in the artifacts and implementation; avoid storing unnecessary raw identifiable transcripts.
- `sources/pensum.json` entries with `url_status` **failed: HTTP 403; empty/short response**: two sources are recorded as failed. Their recorded status is honest, but downstream use must not treat inaccessible sources as verified; fetch a legitimate accessible alternative and record the actual result.
